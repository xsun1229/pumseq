1. Sites

The sites are the 5-fold union sites. The 50 genotyped cell lines are split into 5 disjoint folds of 10 (seed 2026). Within each fold the cell lines’ reads are pooled and sites are called jointly (chi-squared test, FDR < 0.01, rate difference > 0.05, depth >= 20). A site is kept if it is called in at least one fold. Details are on the 5-fold site-calling page.

2. Phenotype

The phenotype is the raw counts, not a ratio. For each site and cell line:

  • C: converted reads at the pU base in the treated library (the pU signal);
  • N = C + T: total reads there (the binomial trial count).

Treated replicates are summed within each cell line. No normalisation or imputation is applied: the model takes the counts directly, so a cell line with no reads at a site simply contributes nothing.

3. Covariates

Principal components of an M-value transform of the counts, computed per site and cell line as

\[M = \log_2 \frac{C + \pi_s}{T + 1 - \pi_s},\]

where \(\pi_s\) is the site’s pooled %pU across cell lines. Using the site’s own rate as the pseudocount keeps sparse entries from being pulled to an arbitrary value. Each site’s M-values are standardised, sites with fewer than 5 covered cell lines or no variation are dropped, and PCA is run across cell lines. The top 20 PCs are stored; the scans sweep 3 to 20.

4. Genotypes

Source. Imputed genotypes for 60 Yoruba (YRI) lymphoblastoid cell lines, downloaded from Zenodo record 3870952, on hg19, lifted to hg38 with GATK LiftoverVcf. They were merged across chr1-22, subset to the 50 sequenced cell lines, renamed to CL##### and converted to PLINK2, as described on the linear model’s data page.
